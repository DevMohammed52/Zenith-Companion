## [v0.12.0] — Bye Bye Fish! Forge Recipe Parity & Accurate Crafting

Hey everyone! A fresh update has just dropped for Zenith Companion.

If you’ve been using the **Forge Planner** or checking item recipe trees recently, you might have noticed some strange advice telling you to stockpile 500 Sardines or Herring just to forge a shield. 

The game officially banished fish from forge recipes in the October update, and Zenith is now 100% caught up with the live servers!

### What's Changed?

* **No More Fish in Forge Recipes (Finally!)**  
  All 252 Forge recipes have been synced directly with the official game API. Over 59 recipes that previously called for hundreds of fish have been updated with their actual metal bar requirements (Mercury Bars, Chromite Bars, Mystic Bars, and more).

* **Mythic Level Requirements Fixed**  
  Mythic gear recipes no longer show an outdated flat level 90 requirement. They now accurately scale between level 90 and 96 to match the exact combat level required to wear them in-game (e.g., *Archdemon* is level 94, *Aqua Reaper* is level 96).

* **Accurate Material Trees & "Where Used" Backlinks**  
  Clicking on items like Sardines or Salmon will no longer claim they are used in high-level forging. Meanwhile, metal bars now accurately show every single weapon, armour piece, and tool they craft.

* **Smarter Forge Profit & Cost Breakdowns**  
  Because the Forge Planner now uses the real metal bar recipes, your material shopping lists, total craft costs, and projected market profits are completely accurate to today's economy.
