# Zenith Companion Patch Notes

- **New Forge Lessons Calculator**: Plan your offline smithing sessions with exact burn times, EXP per hour, and gold costs. It automatically splits training into 4h, 6h, or 8h batches based on your account type and membership.
- **Progression Goal Planner**: Pick your target level up to 100 and queue the cheapest, fastest, or balanced route with one click.
- **Burn vs. Sell Inventory Tool**: Tracks all 91 materials (ores, bars, logs, and mob drops) with live market prices, telling you whether to burn them for EXP or sell them on the market.
- **Other Improvements & Fixes**: Forge setups, queues, and inventories now save per character and switch instantly with your profile. Also updated teleport costs to match live in-game prices, added Cursed/Forsaken EXP penalties (-50%), and sped up profile loading.
